# 4.0 Stage-Design

## 4.1 Construction

### 4.1.1 Round count

Stages will require a minimum of 4 scoring hits, up to a maximum of 32 hits. 


### 4.1.2 Vision barriers

Stages can use walls, barrels, and other items to create impenetrable barriers. Unless otherwise stated, obects over 5 feet tall are considered to be an infinite plane that can not be shot through. 

### 4.1.3 Shooting area

The course must define clear zones from where shooting can take place. Shots taken while standing outside these zones are penalized on a per shot basis. The course may require staying inside the shooting area, but this must be clearly stated in the stage documentation.  

### 4.1.4 Safety

Stage design safety issues, such as allowing targets to be engaged beyond the 180 degree line, or shooting steel targets within 21 feet, shall be avoided. 


### 4.1.5 Freestyle

Stages will always be freestyle unless otherwise noted. No more than one stage per match can require fixed target engagement order or fixed shot positions.
