# 4.0 Course of Fire

## 4.1 Starting the Course of Fire

The Range Officer starts a course of fire using the commands defined in [Chapter 8: Range Commands](08-range-commands.md).

Once the start signal is given, the competitor may draw the firearm and engage targets as specified by the written stage briefing.

> **Draft status:** Additional course-design and stage-procedure rules have not yet been defined.
