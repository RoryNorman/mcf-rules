# 8.0 Range Commands

The following commands shall be given when the competitor is in the designated start position.

## 8.1 Make Ready

The competitor is authorized to draw the firearm, dry fire, practice reloads, take sight pictures, and load the firearm in preparation for the course of fire.

## 8.2 Are You Ready?

Once Make Ready is complete and the competitor has settled into the start position, the Range Officer will ask for confirmation that the competitor is ready. Silence will be interpreted as confirmation.

## 8.3 Standby

Once the competitor is motionless in the start position, the Range Officer will wait approximately 1–3 seconds before giving the start signal.

## 8.4 Start Signal

The start signal may be audible (timer beep) or visual (light). Once the signal is given, the competitor may draw the firearm and engage targets.

> **Draft status:** Commands for completing, stopping, and clearing a course of fire have not yet been defined.
