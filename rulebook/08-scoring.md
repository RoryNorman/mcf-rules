# 7.0 Scoring

## 7.1 Target Scoring

Targets consist of cardboard targets with three distinct scoring zones.

| Zone | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Alpha | 5 |
| Charlie | 3 |
| Delta | 1 |

The Alpha zone may be divided into two smaller scoring areas.

### 7.1.1 Line Breaks

Hits touching a scoring line receive the higher scoring value. This also applies to penalty targets.

## 7.2 Small Capacity Division

Positive scoring hits are doubled.

Examples:

- 1 Alpha = 10 points
- 1 Charlie = 6 points
- 1 Delta = 2 points
