# 9.0 Disqualifications

Disqualification (DQ) removes a competitor from further participation in the match. It is reserved for violations identified by these rules as disqualifying offenses, particularly unsafe firearm or ammunition handling.

## 9.1 Safety Disqualifications

Safety violations identified as disqualifying offenses in [Chapter 3: Safety](03-safety.md) result in a match DQ. These include:

- Inserting ammunition into a firearm outside the course of fire.
- Handling snap caps or empty cartridge cases in a safety area or inserting them into a firearm outside the course of fire.
- Walking around the range with a loaded firearm without proceeding immediately and directly to a match official for supervised unloading.
- Unloading or removing a holstered firearm inside a vehicle on range property.
- Handling ammunition or ammunition containers inside a safety area.

This list summarizes Chapter 3; the complete safety provisions remain controlling.

## 9.2 Responding to a Disqualifying Violation

When a match official observes a suspected disqualifying violation during a course of fire, the official will stop the competitor and direct them through the normal unloading procedure. The firearm must be made safe before the violation is discussed.

The match official should identify the rule violated and notify match leadership. A DQ decision must be communicated clearly and respectfully to the competitor.

## 9.3 Effect of Disqualification

A disqualified competitor may not shoot additional stages. Disqualification does not excuse unsafe handling after the decision; the competitor must continue to follow all range and safety-area rules while on range property.

A DQ does not prevent the competitor from assisting with match operations or observing the match when match leadership and range policy permit it.
