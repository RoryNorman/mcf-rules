# 6.0 Divisions

Divisions group competitors by firearm capacity rather than by individual firearm features. A competitor's division determines the maximum ammunition capacity permitted in each magazine or cylinder and at the start of a course of fire.

Capacity limits are maximums, not required loading levels. A competitor may load fewer rounds than the division permits.

## 6.1 Modern Capacity

Modern Capacity represents contemporary carry setups using higher-capacity firearms.

- Maximum magazine capacity: 25 rounds
- Maximum start condition: 25 rounds in the magazine plus 1 round in the chamber

There are no division-specific restrictions on magwells, ports, compensators, optics, sights, firearm size, firearm weight, or other features. All equipment remains subject to the general equipment and safety rules.

## 6.2 Factory Capacity

Factory Capacity represents typical factory-capacity configurations.

- Maximum magazine capacity: 18 rounds
- Maximum start condition: 18 rounds in the magazine plus 1 round in the chamber

There are no division-specific restrictions on magwells, ports, compensators, optics, sights, firearm size, firearm weight, or other features. All equipment remains subject to the general equipment and safety rules.

## 6.3 Small Capacity

Small Capacity represents lower-capacity firearms, including micro-compact pistols and revolvers.

- Maximum magazine or cylinder capacity: 8 rounds
- Maximum pistol start condition: 8 rounds in the magazine plus 1 round in the chamber
- Maximum revolver start condition: 8 rounds in the cylinder

There are no division-specific restrictions on magwells, ports, compensators, optics, sights, firearm weight, or other features. All equipment remains subject to the general equipment and safety rules.

Positive scoring hits in this division receive the adjustment defined in [Section 8.2](08-scoring.md#82-small-capacity-division).
