# 6.0 Divisions


## Modern Capacity

Representative of modern carry setups. Maximum capacity is 25 rounds in the magazine, with a 25+1 start. No restrictions on magwells, ports, compensators, optics, sights, size, weight, or other features. 

## Factory Capacity

Representative of typical factory configurations. Maximum capacity is 18 rounds in the magazine, with an 18+1 start. No restrictions on magwells, ports, compensators, optics, sights, size, weight, or other features. 

## Small Capacity

Representative of lower capacity firearms such as micro setups, or revolvers. Maximum capacity is 8 rounds in the magazine or cylinder, with an 8+1 start. No restrictions on magwells, ports, compensators, optics, sights, weight, or other features.
