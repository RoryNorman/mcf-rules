# Appendix: Glossary and Examples

## Glossary

**Cold range:** A range where firearms remain unloaded except under a match official's supervision at the firing position.

**DQ:** Disqualification from the match.

**Range Officer:** The match official who supervises a competitor during a course of fire and issues range commands.

**Safety area:** A designated, backstopped area where competitors may handle unloaded firearms subject to the restrictions in [Chapter 3](03-safety.md).

## Scoring Examples

In the Small Capacity division, positive scoring hits are doubled:

- 1 Alpha = 10 points
- 1 Charlie = 6 points
- 1 Delta = 2 points
