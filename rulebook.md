# 1.0 Purpose and Identity

## 1.1 Foundation

MCF is a modernized concealed action pistol sport. MCF emphasizes safety, accessibility, and real-world practicality. It encourages skill development with normal equipment.

> *"Run what you have, see what works, fix what doesn't."*

## 1.2 Spirit of Concealment

MCF has an intrinsic concealed carry focus, emphasizing practical, daily-carry style gear.

Match officials have leeway to be subjective on equipment if they believe the spirit of the sport is being violated, and a culture of community self-policing is encouraged.

## 1.3 Real-World Value

MCF stresses speed and accuracy equally, as these two components together represent the foundation of concealed carry skill.

Having one without the other is a deficiency.

## 1.4 Skills Over Theatrics

Artificial restrictions without real-world value (rigid target priority, magazine retention rules, walkthrough restrictions, etc.) are not part of MCF.

MCF tests shooting skills, not theatrics.

## 1.5 Modern Relevance

MCF stays relevant with frequent updates in the concealed carry world.

Advancements in carry equipment will be permitted by default unless such equipment represents a safety issue or violates the spirit of concealment.

# 2.0 Core Tenets

## 2.1 Safety

MCF has a culture of safety first.

Safety rules are non-negotiable (see Chapters 3 and 9). The serious nature of concealed carry demands the highest standards of weapon handling.

## 2.2 Personal Growth

MCF provides a fun environment to develop concealed carry skills.

Not every stage must be completely serious, but every match should contain an element of skill development.

## 2.3 Equity

Course construction and match operation will ensure a similar experience for every competitor.

When issues of equity arise, match officials will determine what corrections are necessary to restore fairness or remove elements causing inequity.

## 2.4 Sportsmanship

All competitors, including staff, are expected to treat everyone with respect and courtesy.

All participants will display integrity and honesty.

Cheating is not permitted.

## 2.5 Participation

All competitors are expected to help reset stages and assist with match flow.

Volunteers are the foundation of the sport, and gratitude should be shown to those who go above and beyond.

## 2.6 Community

MCF fosters a sense of community at the local, regional, and national levels.

Everyone is welcome in MCF.

New shooters should receive assistance to ensure their safety and success.

# 3.0 Safety

## 3.1 Cold Range

All MCF matches operate as a cold range.

All firearms must remain unloaded until the competitor is under the supervision of a match official giving commands at the firing position.

Cold firearms may only be handled:

- Under official supervision at the firing position, or
- Inside a designated safety area.

### 3.1.1

Firearms must be clear of:

- Magazines
- Moon clips
- Any other loading device

when outside the course of fire.

Hammers must be down, and striker-fired pistols must be in the fired condition.

Anyone outside the course of fire not meeting these conditions will be escorted to a safety area to correct the issue.

If ammunition is inserted into the firearm in any way, the competitor will be disqualified (DQ).

### 3.1.2

Snap caps and empty cartridge cases are considered ammunition.

Handling them in a safety area or inserting them into a firearm will result in a DQ.

### 3.1.3

A competitor who arrives with a loaded firearm will **not** receive a DQ provided they proceed immediately and directly to the nearest match official and unload under supervision in a shooting area.

Walking around the range with a loaded firearm under any other circumstances will result in a DQ.

### 3.1.4

Unloading or removing a holstered firearm inside a vehicle on range property will result in a DQ.

---

## 3.2 Safety Areas

All matches must provide a designated, backstopped safety area where competitors may handle unloaded firearms.

The safety area shall:

- Have clearly marked boundaries.
- Include a surface for resting equipment.

### 3.2.1

Handling ammunition or ammunition containers inside the safety area will result in a DQ.

Examples include:

- Ammunition boxes
- Magazines containing ammunition
- Moon clips
- Speed loaders
- Ammunition cans

### 3.2.2

Ammunition may remain on a competitor's belt, pouch, or in a pocket, provided it is not handled.

### 3.2.3

Larger containers such as:

- Range bags
- Backpacks
- Carts

may be brought into the safety area, provided no ammunition inside them is handled.

### 3.2.4

The following may be handled in the safety area:

- Empty magazines
- Magazine weight inserts

Snap caps and empty cartridge cases are considered ammunition and will result in a DQ.

### 3.2.5

Assembled firearms inside the safety area must always be pointed in a safe direction toward the backstop.

# 4.0 Range Commands

## 4.1 Starting the Course of Fire

The following commands shall be given when the competitor is in the designated start position.

### Make Ready

The competitor is authorized to:

- Draw the firearm
- Dry fire
- Practice reloads
- Take sight pictures
- Load the firearm

in preparation for the course of fire.

### Are You Ready?

Once Make Ready is complete and the competitor has settled into the start position, the Range Officer will ask for confirmation that the competitor is ready.

Silence will be interpreted as confirmation.

### Standby

Once the competitor is motionless in the start position, the Range Officer will wait approximately 1–3 seconds before giving the start signal.

### Start Signal

The start signal may be:

- Audible (timer beep)
- Visual (light)

Once the signal is given, the competitor may draw the firearm and engage targets.

# 8.0 Scoring

## 8.1 Target Scoring

Targets will consist of cardboard targets with three distinct scoring zones.

| Zone | Value |
|------|------:|
| Alpha | 5 |
| Charlie | 3 |
| Delta | 1 |

The Alpha zone may be divided into two smaller scoring areas.

### 8.1.1 Line Breaks

Hits touching a scoring line receive the higher scoring value.

This also applies to penalty targets.

## 8.2 Small Capacity Division

Positive scoring hits are doubled.

Example:

- 1 Alpha = 10 points
- 1 Charlie = 6 points
- 1 Delta = 2 points
